**Design of a Safe Walking Hexapod Robot** (2024) — bio-inspired hexapod design in Simulink, with literature presentations and a LaTeX introduction exercise.

**Quadruped simulation and deep RL** (2025) — eight students working through the locomotion literature (MIT Cheetah 3, RMA, CPG-RL, PMTG, Learning by Cheating, Legs as Manipulator) and building simulation and learning pipelines, with code reviews and documentation.
