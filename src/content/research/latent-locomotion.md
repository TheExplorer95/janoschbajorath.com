An encoder maps the robot's proprioceptive history to a latent $z_t$; a controller maps $z_t$ and the body state to joint actions. Nothing else carries dynamic information or task intent. The project investigates the latent through four lenses — probeability, local continuity, temporal predictability, coverage — and asks whether its evolution across training can drive a curriculum.

See the [WHERE 2026](/research/where-2026) and [SAB 2026](/research/sab-2026) posters for the current state.
